# Singapore C-Corp Registration Execution Checklist

**Status**: In Progress  
**Issue**: #29  
**Based on**: Research from Issue #28 and commit aaeecb7

## Phase 1: Pre-Registration (Week 1)

### 1.1 Company Name Reservation
- [ ] Check name availability at https://www.acra.gov.sg/
- [ ] Reserve company name (SGD 15)
- [ ] Ensure name complies with ACRA guidelines
- **Target Date**: Day 1-2
- **Responsible**: TBD

### 1.2 Document Preparation
- [ ] Gather Director NRIC/Passport copies
- [ ] Gather Director proof of residential address
- [ ] Gather Shareholder NRIC/Passport copies
- [ ] Gather Shareholder proof of residential address
- [ ] Prepare Memorandum and Articles of Association
- [ ] Prepare Director's consent form
- [ ] Prepare Statement of compliance
- **Target Date**: Day 1-2
- **Responsible**: TBD

### 1.3 Local Requirements Setup
- [ ] Identify/appoint resident director (Singapore citizen/PR/Employment Pass holder)
- [ ] Secure registered office address (physical, not PO Box)
- [ ] Consider nominee director services if needed
- **Target Date**: Day 1-2
- **Responsible**: TBD

## Phase 2: ACRA Registration (Week 1)

### 2.1 Submit Registration
- [ ] Submit application via ACRA (https://www.acra.gov.sg/)
- [ ] Pay registration fee (SGD 300-400)
- [ ] Upload all required documents
- **Target Date**: Day 3-4
- **Responsible**: TBD

### 2.2 Receive Confirmation
- [ ] Receive Business Registration Number (UEN)
- [ ] Download Business Profile
- [ ] Verify all details are correct
- **Target Date**: Day 5
- **Responsible**: TBD

## Phase 3: Post-Registration (Week 2)

### 3.1 Company Secretary
- [ ] Appoint Company Secretary (within 6 months of incorporation)
- [ ] Ensure secretary is resident in Singapore
- [ ] Sign secretary consent form
- **Target Date**: Day 6-7
- **Responsible**: TBD

### 3.2 Corporate Bank Account
- [ ] Choose bank (DBS/OCBC/UOB/HSBC/Standard Chartered)
- [ ] Prepare required documents:
  - Business registration certificate
  - Director IDs
  - Proof of address
  - Company resolution for account opening
- [ ] Submit application
- [ ] Activate account
- **Target Date**: Day 8-9
- **Responsible**: TBD

### 3.3 Tax Registration
- [ ] Register with IRAS (https://www.iras.gov.sg/)
- [ ] Apply for GST registration (if annual turnover > SGD 1 million)
- [ ] Understand corporate tax obligations (17% flat rate)
- **Target Date**: Day 10-11
- **Responsible**: TBD

### 3.4 Business Licenses
- [ ] Identify required licenses based on business activity
- [ ] Apply for necessary licenses (NEA/MAS/TradeNet etc.)
- [ ] Pay license fees
- [ ] Receive license approvals
- **Target Date**: Day 12-14
- **Responsible**: TBD

## Phase 4: Compliance Setup

### 4.1 Accounting System
- [ ] Set up accounting software
- [ ] Engage accounting services (SGD 1,000-3,000/year)
- [ ] Establish bookkeeping procedures

### 4.2 Statutory Registers
- [ ] Set up register of members
- [ ] Set up register of directors
- [ ] Set up register of charges
- [ ] Maintain minute books

### 4.3 Annual Compliance Calendar
- [ ] Mark ACRA annual return deadline
- [ ] Mark IRAS tax return deadline
- [ ] Schedule Annual General Meeting
- [ ] Set up compliance reminders

## Notes

- All directors must sign consent forms personally
- Company secretary must be appointed within 6 months
- Annual compliance is mandatory (fines apply for late filing)
- Consider engaging corporate service provider for first registration
