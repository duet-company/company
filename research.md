# Singapore C-Corp Registration Research\n\n## Overview\nSingapore is a leading jurisdiction for company incorporation due to its business-friendly environment, political stability, and strategic location in Asia.\n\n## Registration Process\n\n### 1. ACRA Registration\n- **Agency**: Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA)\n- **Timeline**: 1-3 business days for standard registration\n- **Cost**: SGD 300-400 for standard package\n- **Requirements**:\n  - Company name approval\n  - Local registered address\n  - At least 1 shareholder\n  - At least 1 director (must be a resident of Singapore)\n  - Company secretary (must be appointed within 6 months)\n\n### 2. Required Documents\n- **For Directors**:\n  - NRIC/Passport copy\n  - Proof of residential address\n  - Director's consent form\n- **For Shareholders**:\n  - NRIC/Passport copy\n  - Proof of residential address\n- **Company Documents**:\n  - Memorandum and Articles of Association\n  - Consent to act as director\n  - Statement of compliance\n\n### 3. Post-Registration Requirements\n- **Business License**: Depends on business activity\n- **Tax Registration**:\n  - GST registration (if annual turnover > SGD 1 million)\n  - Corporate tax registration\n- **Bank Account**: Corporate bank account setup\n- **Permits/Licenses**: Industry-specific permits if required\n\n## Timeline Overview\n\n### Week 1: Company Setup\n- Day 1-2: Name reservation and document preparation\n- Day 3-4: ACRA registration submission\n- Day 5: Receive business registration number\n\n### Week 2: Post-Registration\n- Day 6-7: Company secretary appointment\n- Day 8-9: Bank account setup\n- Day 10-11: Tax registration\n- Day 12-14: Business license applications\n\n## Cost Breakdown\n\n### Registration Costs\n- ACRA registration fee: SGD 300-400\n- Name reservation: SGD 15\n- Company secretary services: SGD 500-800/year\n- Registered office address: SGD 300-600/year\n\n### Additional Costs\n- Corporate bank account setup: SGD 50-200\n- Business licenses: Varies by industry\n- Accounting services: SGD 1,000-3,000/year\n\n## Government Agencies\n\n1. **ACRA**: Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority\n   - Website: https://www.acra.gov.sg/\n   - Role: Company registration and regulation\n\n2. **IRAS**: Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore\n   - Website: https://www.iras.gov.sg/\n   - Role: Tax registration and compliance\n\n3. **SLA**: Singapore Land Authority\n   - Website: https://www.sla.gov.sg/\n   - Role: Registered address verification\n\n4. **Industry-Specific Agencies**:\n   - MOM (Ministry of Manpower) for employment matters\n   - NEA (National Environment Agency) for food establishments\n   - etc.\n\n## Local Requirements\n\n### Resident Director\n- Must be a Singapore citizen, permanent resident, or Employment Pass holder\n- Responsible for ensuring company compliance with Singapore laws\n\n### Company Secretary\n- Must be a natural person resident in Singapore\n- Appointed within 6 months of incorporation\n- Responsible for maintaining statutory registers and filing requirements\n\n### Registered Office\n- Must be a physical address in Singapore\n- Cannot be a PO Box\n- Used for official communications\n\n## Post-Registration Setup\n\n### Bank Account Setup\n- Required documents: Business registration certificate, director IDs, proof of address\n- Timeline: 1-2 weeks\n- Recommended banks: DBS, OCBC, UOB (local), HSBC, Standard Chartered (international)\n\n### Tax Registration\n- **Corporate Tax**: Flat rate of 17% on chargeable income\n- **GST**: 8% (as of 2023), applicable if turnover exceeds SGD 1 million\n- **Filing Requirements**:\n  - Annual tax return\n  - GST returns (if registered)\n  - Financial statements\n\n### Business Licenses\n- Depends on business activity:\n  - Food establishment: NEA license\n  - Financial services: MAS license\n  - Import/Export: TradeNet registration\n  - etc.\n\n## Compliance Requirements\n\n### Annual Requirements\n- File annual returns with ACRA\n- Hold annual general meeting\n- Maintain proper accounting records\n- File annual tax returns\n\n### Ongoing Compliance\n- Keep statutory registers updated\n- Maintain proper accounting records\n- Comply with employment laws (if hiring)\n- Renew licenses as required\n\n## Recommendations\n\n1. **Use Incorporation Service**: Consider using a corporate service provider for first-time registration\n2. **Plan for Resident Director**: Ensure you have a suitable resident director or consider nominee services\n3. **Budget for Compliance**: Factor in ongoing compliance costs beyond initial registration\n4. **Consider Tax Planning**: Singapore offers various tax incentives for startups and certain industries\n\n## Next Steps\n\n1. **Prepare Documents**: Gather all required documents for registration\n2. **Choose Service Provider**: Select between direct ACRA registration or corporate service provider\n3. **Reserve Company Name**: Check and reserve desired company name\n4. **Complete Registration**: Submit application to ACRA\n5. **Post-Registration Setup**: Complete all post-registration requirements\n\n---\n\n*Research completed on April 22, 2026*
