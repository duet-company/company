# Company Registration Process

## Overview
Registration of Duet Company as a Singapore C-Corporation

## Steps

### 1. Business Structure Selection
- Choose C-Corporation structure
- Understand tax implications
- Define shareholder structure

### 2. Company Name Reservation
- Reserve "Duet Company Pte. Ltd."
- Check name availability
- Secure name for 120 days

### 3. Document Preparation
- Prepare Memorandum & Articles of Association
- Prepare director and shareholder details
- Prepare registered office address

### 4. Company Registration
- File with Accounting & Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA)
- Pay registration fees (SGD 300)
- Obtain Unique Entity Number (UEN)

### 5. Post-Registration Requirements
- Open corporate bank account
- Register for taxes (GST, corporate tax)
- Obtain necessary licenses
- Set up accounting system

## Timeline
- Week 1: Document preparation and name reservation
- Week 2: Company registration and post-registration setup

## Dependencies
- Director identification documents
- Shareholder information
- Registered office address

## Success Criteria
- Company successfully registered with ACRA
- UEN obtained
- Bank account opened
- Legal documents completed
